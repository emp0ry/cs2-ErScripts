ErScripts has a precise technical character: a mathematical reticle assembled from stable points, rotated coordinate planes and interrupted radial arcs. The composition is calculated, rather than a 3D asset or a simulated game overlay. It responds gently to the pointer, arrow keys and independent click waves. A calm 24-second loop has a static reduced-motion pose and SVG graphics fallback. Rendering pauses offscreen/hidden and releases resources on disposal. No visible interaction instructions or background-pause control.

Make the product concrete through authentic, modestly sized captures. The source captures are small: do not present them as HD footage or crop away their edges. Clips play only on request, can pause, and stop when hidden/offscreen. A shared compact stage holds keystrokes, crosshair, bomb timer and settings, with concise captions and a full screenshot viewer. No working mini-app, global key capture or imitation native settings.

Tell the story through an asymmetric hero, authentic capture stage, three editorial feature families, a compact architecture diagram and a useful Windows setup guide. Keep the launch-options string exact and copyable. All primary content and fallback links live in semantic HTML. Preserve #top, #features, #motion, #how and #responsibility, the existing domain and icon URL.
